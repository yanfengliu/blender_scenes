# Sailor character guide-flow continuation

The user authorized continued work after the previous draft remained visually rejected. The prior revision and references are preserved. Root owns the integrated candidate; bounded hair and collar implementations occupy separate files, with independent visual review after rendering.

The earlier assumption that long01 atlas V follows hair growth proved false: two base guide banks contain approximately 45 mm jumps and 94 degree turns near the crown. The head-width clamp then synchronized further bends at two fixed heights. Neither more strands nor a passing collision gate repaired those shapes. The new groom therefore uses continuous anatomical paths; `tools/check_realistic_character.py` retains its independently defined true surface-intersection check.

The rear skirt band has a separate measured cause: the loft's rearward slope changes .094 to .961 to .154 m/m. A continuous waist-to-hem flare with 64 rows replaces that compressed transition. Curved quad scarf tails replace single planar polygons. Source-defined skin tint, lash/brow shadow settings, and specular settings are restored. Conservative eye-opening and mouth-corner morph adjustments remain topology-preserving.

The projected collar repair failed twice: changing ray direction caused visible tears, and adaptive projection failed its 5 mm edge / 2.24 mm sampled-clearance bound after seven refinements. The accepted geometric replacement clips the single evaluated blouse surface and offsets its interpolated normals, with opaque ivory bands on the same mesh. Moving the inner-bound transition behind the front neck removed the V opening's hourglass kink.

Adding more separate fringe did not remove the overhanging cap edge. A unified crown-rooted guide family spanning forehead to rear scalp did. Ear penetration had another cause: inside-out rays stopped at skull before the outer ear. Outside-in rays and neighboring-angle clearance remove the measured intersections. Sock face-center selection left jagged garment tops; clipping the actual polygons fixed that but exposed a skin cut. Extending body skin below the opaque cuff resolved the visible holes. This repeats the neck-cut defect class rather than creating a material problem.

Five integrated candidates were attempted in this authorized continuation, including one pre-render failure and one diagnostic with the frozen stable collar. Final candidate 5 is saved at `outputs/sailor_character_finish/`, with 128-sample hero, portrait, profile, and back images. Primary and independent review of each native image confirms the V/cuff fixes and no additional visible geometry breaks. Skin, hair, clothing, and pose remain more uniform/stiff than the references, so the model is an editable approximation and CHR-001 remains open. The exact bytes and source hashes are recorded in the ignored review/manifest; no code commit or push was made.
